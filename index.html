The 5-Level Business Intelligence Architecture

Framework established during the masterclasses: transitioning from data dumps to high-leverage strategic actions.

L1

Level 1: Key Performance Indicators (What is happening?)

The institution processes 4,269 total applications with a baseline approval rate of 62.2%. Total capital requested exceeds ₹6,462 Crores, with an average applicant CIBIL score of 600.

L2

Level 2: Trends & Distributions (Where are patterns moving?)

Tenure analysis reveals approval stability in loans between 2 and 8 years. Applicants with longer terms (12-20 years) show higher sensitivity to interest rate variance. A distinct bifurcation exists at CIBIL = 600.

L3

Level 3: Decision Drivers (Why is it happening?)

Feature importance analysis demonstrates that CIBIL score accounts for 83.5% of decision weight, followed by loan tenure duration (6.5%), income leverage ratio (4.0%), and total collateral assets (2.1%).

L4

Level 4: Risks & Opportunities (What could go wrong & where to expand?)

Portfolio Risk: 37.8% subprime concentration without liquid asset collateral creates elevated NPA threat.
Portfolio Opportunity: 28% of borrowers hold total assets > ₹1 Crore with CIBIL > 750, representing an underserved prime demographic.

L5

Level 5: Prescriptive Actions (What must the business execute?)

1. Straight-Through Processing: 100% automated instant approval for CIBIL ≥ 750 and LTI ≤ 3.5.
2. Risk-Based Pricing Grid: Tier 1 (8.25%), Tier 2 (9.50%), Tier 3 (11.75% + 130% collateral).
3. Credit Rehabilitation Routing: Automatic decline for CIBIL < 550 with referral to credit counseling partners.

Model Explainability & Driver Hierarchy

Relative feature influence calculated via Gini impurity reduction across the decision forest.

1. CIBIL Credit Score (83.5% Impact)

The single strongest predictor of loan repayment. Historical data reveals that scores above 650 maintain a 98% non-default record.

2. Loan Tenure (6.5% Impact)

Extended tenures introduce compounding macroeconomic risk and interest rate exposure.

3. Income Leverage / LTI (4.0% Impact)

Borrowers requesting more than 4.5x their verified salary face cash flow constraints during inflation spikes.
